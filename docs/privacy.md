# Chillar privacy notice (draft v2026-10)

> Draft for product and legal review. Not legal advice. Written to the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 and the DPDP Rules, 2025.

## Who we are

Chillar is a family money-and-learning app. The **parent** who sets up the family is the account holder and, for every child profile, the person who gives consent (the DPDP Act treats everyone under 18 as a child).

## What we collect and why

| Data | Why | Required |
|---|---|---|
| Parent name, mobile number, city | Sign-in, alerts, support | Yes |
| Child first name, age band, avatar | Adapt lessons and the kid experience to the child's age | Yes |
| Pocket-money ledger (allowance, chores, jar balances, goals) | The core product | Yes |
| Learning progress (lessons, quiz scores, streaks, XP) | Progress, levels and parent reports | Yes |
| Chore photos | Proof of a finished chore, shown only to the family | Optional, per chore |
| School link (attendance, report cards) | Verified achievement rewards | Optional, opt-in, not yet live |
| Personalised-ads preference on parent screens | Only affects the parent's own screens | Optional, opt-in |

We do **not** collect precise location, contacts, biometrics, or advertising identifiers from children, and we never sell personal data.

## Children

* Kid screens never show personalised ads and never transmit advertising identifiers. Kid screens may show contextual ads unless the family is on Premium; there are no ads inside lessons for children under 9.
* No prices, offers or purchases are shown to children. Premium content shows "ask a parent".
* The daily kid-mode time limit is set by the parent and enforced in the app.
* Chat-style features (AI coach) will be age-gated and reviewed before launch.

## Consent

Consent is recorded with a notice version and timestamp. Verifiable parental consent via DigiLocker is planned for the pilot; the current build records an OTP-style consent. When the notice changes materially, families are asked again.

## Your rights

From Settings a parent can **export** a complete JSON copy of the family's data and **delete** the family and every record. Requests can also be sent to the grievance officer below. We respond within the timelines the DPDP Rules require.

## Storage and security

Data is stored on the device; with cloud sync enabled it is stored in India-region infrastructure, scoped to the family with row-level security, encrypted in transit and at rest. Parent PINs are never exported.

## Third parties

App-store billing (Apple, Google), cloud hosting, and, where a family opts in, partner offers shown on parent screens. A list of processors will be published before public launch.
